# Reviewing AI profiles

Every review you submit becomes training data for Evolve's own profile model.
What teaches the model the most is **the reason you give when you disagree**.

## Where

Admin → **AI Profiles**, **User AI Profiles** or **Verification** → **view** → **review**.

## How

1. Open the portfolio and resume using the links at the top of the modal. Review against the source, not from memory.
2. For each field, choose **✓ agree** or **✕ disagree**.
3. When you disagree, fill in:
   - **Should be:** the correct value, written the same way the AI writes it (e.g. `Early`, `Packaging Design (Practiced)`, `3/5`).
   - **Gap type:**
     - *Missed info:* it's in the source, but the AI missed it.
     - *Made up:* there's no evidence for it in the source.
     - *Overrated:* higher than the evidence supports.
     - *Underrated:* lower than the evidence supports.
     - *Wrong interpretation:* the AI read the evidence but drew the wrong conclusion.
     - *Bad input:* the AI couldn't see the content (image-only pages, broken link, unreadable PDF).
     - *Wording:* the idea is right but it's badly phrased or in the wrong field.
   - **Severity:** *Minor* means a recruiter would still get the right picture. *Major* means it changes how the person comes across. *Wrong* means it would mislead.
   - **Reason (required):** *why* the correct value is correct, citing the evidence.
   - **Evidence:** where you found it (case study name, page, resume section).
4. If a list (skills, traits, strong/growth zones) leaves something out, use **+ missing**.
5. Give an **overall accuracy** score from 1 to 5. Use **mark the rest as agree** only for fields you actually checked.
6. Click **submit review**. Your draft saves automatically, so closing the modal is safe.

## Writing a good reason

| ✗ Not useful | ✓ Useful |
|---|---|
| AI is wrong | Only internships totalling 1 yr, no full-time role, so Early not Mid-level |
| Should be higher | 3 of 4 case studies open with business goals and KPIs (Café rebrand: +18% footfall) |
| Missing skill | Packaging shown in 2 projects (Tea box, Spice jar): Practiced, not absent |
| Bad | Portfolio is image-only Behance; AI saw no text. Process exists in the images |

Rules of thumb: state facts from the source, keep it to 1–2 sentences, and put any general pattern in the overall notes.
